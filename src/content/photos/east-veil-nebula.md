The eastern arc of the Veil Nebula (NGC 6992, Caldwell 33) in Cygnus, part of the remnant of a star that went supernova thousands of years ago. The filaments are where the blast wave is running into the surrounding gas, with hydrogen showing red and oxygen blue-green.
