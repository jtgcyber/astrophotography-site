The first time I've had the scope out in over a year, so this night was as much about blowing the dust off the gear and getting my eye back in as anything else.

A second go at IC 405, this time from the back garden in Glasgow rather than Campbeltown, on the night of 11–12 November 2026. The target climbed from 58° to nearly 69° before a meridian flip at 02:15, then sank into the city glow to the west.

First night with the Baader Neodymium filter. Some thin haze passed through between about 01:30 and 02:30, but those subs still improved the stack, so I kept 111 of the 113.
