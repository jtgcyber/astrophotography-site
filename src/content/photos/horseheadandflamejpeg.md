Two nebulae next to Alnitak in Orion's Belt, about 1,375 light-years away. The Horsehead (Barnard 33) is dark dust in front of the glowing hydrogen of IC 434. The Flame Nebula (NGC 2024) is emission nebula crossed by dark lanes of foreground dust.
