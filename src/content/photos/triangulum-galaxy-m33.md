The third-largest galaxy in the Local Group after Andromeda and the Milky Way, about 2.73 million light-years away. M33 is a face-on spiral with loose arms full of star-forming regions. The largest, NGC 604, is far bigger than the Orion Nebula.

It's 10 hours of data and still the image I'm most proud of.
