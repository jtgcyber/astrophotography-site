The whole Veil Nebula in Cygnus, the remnant of a supernova thousands of years ago. It covers about 3° of sky, so this is several panels stitched together. It takes in the Western Veil (NGC 6960, the Witch's Broom), the Eastern Veil (NGC 6992) and Pickering's Triangle between them.
