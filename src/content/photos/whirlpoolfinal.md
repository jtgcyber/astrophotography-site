A grand-design spiral in Canes Venatici, about 23 million light-years away, interacting with its smaller companion NGC 5195. It was the first galaxy in which spiral structure was seen, by Lord Rosse in 1845. The encounter is compressing gas in M51's arms and setting off star formation.
